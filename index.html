A letter from Anni

Dear Kakka,

I’m really sorry for the things I said and for hurting you.

I know that sometimes when I’m angry or upset, I can say things without thinking about how much they might hurt you, and I genuinely regret that.

I just want you to know that none of those words change how much I love you or how much I care about you.

You mean so much to me, and no silly fight or argument could ever change that.

You’re my sister, and no matter how much we annoy each other, argue, or get mad at each other, I’ll always care about you.

I don’t want you to ever think that one bad moment could make me stop loving you or being there for you.

I’m not expecting you to just forget everything and be okay immediately. I just really wanted to say that I’m sorry, and that I mean it.

I love you so, so much, Kakka. More than I probably say enough. ❤️

Love,
Anni
